# Security

Please report security problems privately through
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/ph4mHoa/qbd-agent-kit/security/advisories/new),
not in a public issue. Include the version, what an attacker could do, and steps to reproduce, without real company
data.

What the kit protects against, and what it leaves to you, your AI app and QuickBooks, is described in
[docs/safety.md](docs/safety.md).
